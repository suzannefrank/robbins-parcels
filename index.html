Two holdings

One portfolio, two personalities

The northern cluster sits along Hamer Road; the southern block is a single connected sweep of farmland. They’re about a mile apart and do not touch — buy together or in pieces.

Hamer Road Cluster

33.83 acres

Four parcels along Hamer Road: 190 Hamer (10.54 ac in two parts), an adjoining 22.26-acre farm, a vacant 0.78-acre lot at 221 Hamer, and a quarter-acre homesite with a 1960 bungalow. Road frontage, Washington Township, Belle Vernon Area schools.

Perryopolis Block

120.14 acres

Three adjoining parcels forming one contiguous block: a 72.98-acre farm with an 1806 brick farmhouse on Redstone Church Rd, a 30.16-acre middle piece tying the block together, and a 17-acre Clean & Green parcel near Perryopolis.

Why this land

What makes it worth a look

🌳

Real scale, real contiguity

120+ acres in a single connected block south of Perryopolis — the kind of assemblage that rarely comes together in Fayette County — plus a separate 34-acre road-frontage cluster.

🏰

An 1806 brick farmhouse

The Redstone Church Rd farm carries a two-story brick residence built in 1806 (~1,760 sq ft) — genuine early-American character on working farmland.

🛰

Road frontage & homesites

The Hamer cluster fronts Hamer Road with a vacant residential lot and a 1960 bungalow homesite — immediate use while the acreage works around it.

🌱

Clean & Green enrollment

The 17-acre Jefferson Township parcel is enrolled in Pennsylvania’s Clean & Green program (ACT 319), carrying reduced agricultural-use tax assessment.
